More Than Fostering

Every dog on this ranch needs more hands than we have.

Not everyone can bring a dog home right now, and that's alright. Second Chance Fosters runs on people who show up in a dozen different ways: driving a dog to the vet, stacking bags of kibble in the barn, writing a check that covers a week of heartworm treatment. If you've got an hour, a truck bed, or a spare room, we've got a place for you.

Ways to Pitch In

Fostering is the biggest need on this ranch, but it isn't the only one. Pick whatever fits your week.

Foster a Dog

Open your home for a few weeks or a few months. We cover food, vetting, and supplies. You cover the couch space.

See dogs waiting →

Volunteer at the Ranch

Help with kennel cleaning, dog walks, and adoption events. Most shifts run Saturday mornings, no experience needed.

Donate Supplies

Crates, leashes, blankets, and bags of kibble go straight into a foster home the same week they arrive.

Sponsor a Dog's Care

Cover a vet bill, a round of vaccines, or a month of food for a dog waiting on a foster placement.

Transport a Dog

Drive one leg of a rescue transport. Sometimes it's an hour to the vet, sometimes it's twenty minutes down the highway.

Spread the Word

Share a foster dog's story with your neighbors, your church group, your softball league. Word of mouth finds real homes.

Starting an Application Takes Ten Minutes

Whichever role you're leaning toward, the process starts the same way. Here's what happens after you reach out.

  1. 01

    Fill Out the Form

    Tell us what you're interested in, foster, volunteer, or something else. Five minutes, no pressure.

  2. 02

    A Quick Phone Call

    Someone from our team calls to talk through your home, your schedule, and what kind of dog might fit best.

  3. 03

    A Home Visit

    A short, friendly walkthrough of your space. We're checking for fencing and safety, not tidiness.

  4. 04

    You're In

    Once you're approved, we start matching you with a dog, a shift, or a project that fits your life.
