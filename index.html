Our Story

We started in a two-stall barn with four dogs nobody else wanted.

That was eleven years ago. A litter of hound mixes had been left at a county shelter that was already over capacity, and the clock was running out. We took them home, patched them up, and found them families within a month. We haven't stopped since.

Today we're an all-volunteer network with no physical shelter, which we think is a strength, not a limitation. Every dog in our care lives in a real home, learns real house manners, and gets to know what a family actually feels like before we ever place them for good.

We're based out of Millbrook, but our foster homes stretch across three counties. If you've got a spare bedroom, a fenced yard, or just a soft spot for a dog with a rough start, there's a place for you here.

What fostering with us actually looks like

Fostering shouldn't cost you money or leave you guessing. Here's what we take care of, so you can focus on the dog in front of you.

Every supply, covered

Crate, leash, bowls, bed, and a bag of food waiting on your porch before the dog even arrives. You never pay out of pocket for basics.

Full vet care, our dime

Vaccines, spay or neuter, dental work, whatever comes up. Our partner vets in Millbrook and Dover handle it, no bill lands on you.

A real person, on call

Every foster gets paired with a coordinator who answers texts at 9pm about a dog who won't stop pacing. We've been there too.

Honest timelines

Most dogs are placed in four to eight weeks. We tell you upfront what to expect and never leave you fostering indefinitely without a plan.

Adopter screening we handle

Applications, home checks, reference calls, all of it runs through our team. You just tell us how the dog does with kids, cats, and car rides.

No dog left behind

If a placement falls through, the dog comes back to us, never to the shelter. That promise is the whole reason we exist.

How it works

From application to a dog on your couch

01

Apply online

A short form about your home, your yard, and any pets you already have. Takes about ten minutes.

02

Quick home chat

One of our coordinators calls to talk through your household and match you with a dog who'll actually fit.

03

Bring your dog home

We deliver the dog with everything they need and walk you through the first few days in person.

04

We stay close

Check-ins, vet scheduling, adopter matching. When it's time to say goodbye, you'll know you helped write a good chapter.
