How it works

From a quiet
application
to a full house.

Fostering isn't complicated, but we don't wing it either. Every dog that comes through Second Chance Fosters follows the same steady process, so you always know what's next and so does the dog. Here's what that actually looks like, start to finish.

5

simple steps

The process, step by step

No hidden fees, no surprise home inspections with a clipboard. Just five stages we've refined over years of matching dogs with the right couches.

01

Fill out the foster form

Tell us about your household: other pets, kids, work hours, whether you rent or own. It takes about ten minutes and nothing here locks you in. It just helps us understand where a dog might fit.

02

Talk it through with us

Our foster coordinator calls within a couple days. We ask real questions about your routine and answer yours about vet costs, supplies, and what happens if it isn't a fit. No pressure, no script.

03

Get matched with a dog

We look at energy level, size, temperament, and whatever quirks the dog has, then match against your home. A retired greyhound goes to a quiet apartment. A young herding mix goes somewhere with a yard and time to run.

04

Meet at the sanctuary

Before anyone brings a dog home, you come out and meet them at our property in Millbrook. Walk them around the paddock, see how they settle. Both sides get to say yes, and both sides can say not yet.

05

Bring them home, with backup

We send you home with food, a leash, bedding, and vet records. Our support line is answered by an actual person, day or night, for the whole length of the foster. You're never doing this alone.

"I called with a list of worries and by the end of it I felt like I'd talked to a friend who happened to know everything about dogs. Three days later Biscuit was asleep on my kitchen floor like he'd always lived there."

Renata H., first-time foster, Poughkeepsie
